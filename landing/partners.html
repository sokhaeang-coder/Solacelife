How the referral fee works

New partners start on a flat $300 per retained client for the first 6 months — a trial period so you can see the value of introductions before committing long term. After 6 months, partners who continue move to an industry-standard percentage based on their specialty based on the standard referral rate for your industry. We're also open to custom agreements tailored to your practice. All rates are agreed in writing before your first referral. No conversion, no fee. Ever.

A $300 referral fee on a $5,000 estate plan is 6% of the first engagement — and the full relationship is yours from that point forward.

Common questions

Will you share my client's data?

Never without explicit consent. We are PIPEDA-compliant and built with Canadian privacy law as a foundation. We introduce the client to you — we don't share their Solace Life data with anyone.

Can I decline a referral?

Yes, always. You are never obligated to take an introduction. If a referred client isn't a fit for your practice, simply let us know and no fee applies.

What happens after the 6-month trial?

The $300 flat fee covers your first 6 months — a trial period to experience the quality of introductions. After that, partners who continue move to the standard referral rate for their industry. We're also open to custom agreements based on your practice. All rates are discussed and agreed in writing well in advance. Nothing changes without your signature.

What if the client doesn't retain me?

No fee. The $300 applies only once a client formally engages your services. Introductions that don't convert cost you nothing — no exceptions.
