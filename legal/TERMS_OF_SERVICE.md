## 1. What Solace Life Is — and Is Not

Solace Life is a digital service for **storing and sharing copies of personal
information**: photos, videos, voice recordings, written messages, and digital copies
of documents you choose to save ("Content").

**Solace Life is NOT:**

- **A will, trust, or estate planning instrument.** Nothing you do in Solace Life —
  including choosing which family members can see which documents — transfers
  ownership of money, property, assets, or belongings to anyone. Access settings in
  the app control *who can view copies of information*, and nothing more.
- **A substitute for legal, financial, or medical advice.** Consult licensed
  professionals for estate planning, financial decisions, and healthcare directives.
- **An official record.** Documents stored in Solace Life are copies for the
  convenience of you and your family. Original documents, wherever they are held,
  remain the authoritative versions.

## 2. Vault Sharing Is Informational Only

When your vault becomes visible to your designated family members (the "release"),
they receive **viewing access to copies of information** — nothing else. A family
member's ability to see a document (for example, a property title, bank account
details, or a will) does **not** mean, and must not be interpreted to mean, that:

- they have been given the underlying asset, account, or property;
- they have been named a beneficiary, executor, or trustee;
- the deceased intended any transfer of ownership to them.

Any actual transfer of assets is governed exclusively by applicable law and valid
legal instruments (wills, trusts, beneficiary designations, etc.) outside of
Solace Life.

## 3. Trusted Contacts, Emergency Contacts, and Release

- You designate trusted contacts and choose which sections of your vault each person
  may see. Sections from which you remove everyone remain sealed and are shown to
  no one.
- Designated contacts must accept their role by email confirmation before it takes
  effect.
- Vault release is triggered by your designated contacts confirming your passing (or
  by your missed check-in escalation process, where enabled). **You acknowledge that
  Solace Life relies on the good faith of the people you designate.** Solace Life is
  not responsible for false, premature, or delayed confirmations made by your
  designated contacts, and does not independently verify death.
- Release makes Content visible according to your settings as they existed at the
  time of release. It is your responsibility to keep your access choices current.

## 5. Security and Its Limits

Certain sensitive fields (passwords, account numbers, private notes) are encrypted
on your device before upload. You acknowledge that:

- encryption keys for these fields exist only on your device(s); if your device is
  lost or reset, encrypted fields may become permanently unreadable, including to
  your family after release;
- no system is perfectly secure, and you store Content at your own discretion.
