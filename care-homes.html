Questions directors ask us

What does this cost the facility or residents?

Nothing. The pilot is free, no payment information is collected, and there is no obligation for residents, families, or the facility — during the pilot or after it.

Is this a sales program aimed at our residents?

No. No prices are shown or discussed with residents, and there is no follow-up sales contact. The pilot exists to make the product better for this generation — that's the entire transaction.

What happens to a resident's recordings if they stop?

Whatever they choose. They can keep their account, hand it to family, or ask us to delete everything — which we do immediately and confirm in writing.

Do residents need to share passwords or financial information?

No. Recording and sending memories requires none of that. The app does include an optional vault feature, but it is never demonstrated unsolicited and is not part of the pilot's asks.

What if a resident passes away during or after the pilot?

Their recordings are preserved and released only to the family members they chose. The release process relies on the resident's designated trusted contacts confirming the passing — Solace Life does not independently verify death. Solace Life shares copies of information only — it is not a will and transfers no assets.

Can our staff observe sessions?

We'd be disappointed if they didn't. All sessions are in common areas and staff are welcome at every one.
